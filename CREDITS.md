# Credits & Upstream Attributions

`bundle-useful-skills` is an independent, open-source skill router, bundle manifest system, and runtime orchestrator. We stand on the shoulders of giants. This repository exists to provide rigorous governance, supply-chain verification, and intelligent activation for agent skills developed across the open-source and software engineering ecosystem.

Inclusion of upstream skills in this registry does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or direct affiliation. All skills remain the intellectual property of their respective creators and retain their original licenses.
