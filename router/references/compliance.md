# Compliance Gate

Read only when a route flags regulated claims, personal data, or an audience that needs protecting. This gate cannot be disabled once triggered.

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Where the answer is unclear or the exposure is material, the outcome is "route to counsel", not a best guess.

## Triggers

Any of the following activates this pass:

- Health, medical, wellness, or supplement claims.
- Financial products, investment, insurance, credit, or earnings claims.
- Personal data collection, tracking, profiling, or cross-border transfer.
- Testimonials, reviews, endorsements, influencer or affiliate content.
- Audiences including minors, or products that appeal to them.
- Regulated categories: gambling, alcohol, tobacco and vaping, pharmaceuticals, weapons, crypto assets.
- Before-and-after imagery, guaranteed outcomes, or superiority claims.

## Claims

- Every factual and comparative claim carries a substantiation record: the source, its date, and what it actually measured. A claim whose evidence cannot be produced is removed, not softened.
- Superlatives ("best", "fastest", "number one") are comparative claims and need comparative evidence, including the comparison set and the date.
- Outcome and earnings claims need a typical-result basis, not a best case. If typical results are unknown, the claim does not run.
- Do not restate a claim as a question or a customer quote to escape substantiation. The obligation follows the message, not the grammar.

## Personal data

- Name the lawful basis for each collection, and collect only what the stated purpose needs.
- Consent must be a positive action, separable per purpose, and as easy to withdraw as to give. Pre-ticked boxes and bundled consent are not consent.
- Marketing and analytics tracking is a separate purpose from service delivery. Do not fire non-essential tags before consent.
- Record retention periods, and a deletion path that actually runs.
- Cross-border transfer needs a stated mechanism. Note where the data physically goes, including through processors and ad platforms.

## Disclosure

- Paid, sponsored, affiliate, and gifted relationships are disclosed prominently, in the same medium and language as the message, before the endorsement — not in a bio, a hashtag block, or a footer.
- Testimonials must be genuine, obtained with permission, and representative. Editing for length must not change meaning.
- Never fabricate a review, testimonial, endorsement, case study, or metric, and never publish generated imagery of a real person or organization as if it were documentary.
- AI-generated content that could be mistaken for a real person, event, or record is labelled.

## Minors and vulnerable audiences

- If the audience includes minors, behavioural targeting and data collection are off by default.
- Do not use urgency, scarcity, or loss-framing pressure against audiences in a vulnerable moment — debt, illness, bereavement, addiction.

## Platform and sector rules

Ad platforms and app stores impose their own restrictions, often stricter than law, and enforce by removal without appeal. Check the destination platform's policy for the specific category before production, not after rejection.

## Output

State, in the deliverable's record:

- Which triggers fired.
- The substantiation source and date for each claim that survived.
- Which claims were cut, and why.
- Whether anything needs legal review before publication, and what specifically.

An unresolved compliance question blocks publication. Escalate it; do not route around it.
